# DeePiCt Supervised Training Experiments

All runs train an actin segmentation model on synthetic cryo-ET data generated by the PolNet/FakET pipeline, with checkpoint and output saved to `data/training/out/deepict/run{N}/`.

Config files: `configs/deepict/deepict_run{N}.toml`  
Training script: `pipeline_scripts/supervised_training.py`  
SLURM script: `sbatch slurm_scripts/sbatch_supervised_training.sh configs/deepict/deepict_run{N}.toml`

---

## Run 12

**Dataset:** `deepict_dataset_4` (20 tomograms, 4 conditions)  
**Conditions:** [0, 1, 2]  
**Train:** 42 tomograms (14 per condition)  
**Val:** 12 tomograms (4 per condition)  
**Test:** 6 tomograms (2 per condition)  
**Loss:** DiceLoss (default)  
**lr:** 4e-4 | **batch_size:** 4 | **n_iterations:** 50,000

Baseline run on deepict_dataset_4. Distractor condition 3 excluded.
